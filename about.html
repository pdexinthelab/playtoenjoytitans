About

PlayToEnjoy Titans is an independent games studio making accessible games, designed blind and partially sighted first.

Every game is built so it can be played by ear from start to finish: speaking menus, sound that tells you where everything is, and controls that work on a keyboard, a game controller or a phone. Our main sponsor is Blind and Bougie.

The games so far are Boujee Island, Boujee Olympics, Footy Racer and Num Num Run, with Footy 27 on the way. See all the games.
