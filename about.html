About

PlayToEnjoy Titans is an independent games studio, and a group venture, making games for everyone: sighted and blind gamers can play them together or on their own. Our motto is “No gamers left behind.”

Our games are full-fledged games, not audio-only games. The audio features, such as spoken menus and sounds that tell you where everything is, are built in, and you can switch them on or off from the menus. The controls work on a keyboard, a game controller or a phone.

Our games so far are Palla della Morte (Death Ball), PlayToEnjoy Titans Air Hockey, Boujee Olympics, Footy Racer and Num Num Run, with Boujee Island and Footy 27 on the way. See all the games.

Our story

Every member of our team is a blind or visually impaired coder, and a gaming fan first. For years we waited for someone to make the games we wanted to play, and to speak up for blind and visually impaired gamers. Nobody did. So we took things into our own hands.

We build the games we always wanted to play, so that blind and visually impaired gamers get the same chance as everyone else: to play with friends, family, brothers and sisters, or whoever they meet when they jump into a game. With every release we are building a community around our games, where everyone gets a seat at the table.

No gamers left behind.

Meet the team

The people behind PlayToEnjoy Titans. The links open their own websites.

  • PDex

    Game developer

    PDex, also known as PdexInTheLab, is one of the members of Blind and Boujee and the developer behind Boujee Olympics, Footy Racer and Num Num Run, and the games on the way, Boujee Island and Footy 27, all made with Blind and Boujee. He also co-created the Super Video Golf Accessible Mod.

  • Chris Douglas

    Game maker and musician

    Chris Douglas is a Philadelphia creator of music, food reviews, live streams and accessible games that blind and sighted players enjoy together. Chris made Palla della Morte (Death Ball) and PlayToEnjoy Titans Air Hockey, and co-created the Super Video Golf Accessible Mod.

    Visit Chris Douglas Music

  • Blind and Boujee

    Music duo

    Blind and Boujee is a music duo, a blind poet and a blind producer, blending trap, R&B, drill, African grooves and global rhythms into a sound all their own. Both members are part of our team, and Blind and Boujee is behind every game PDex makes: Boujee Olympics, Footy Racer and Num Num Run, Boujee Island and Footy 27 on the way, and the Super Video Golf Accessible Mod.
