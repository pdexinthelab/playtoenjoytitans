Accessibility statement

No gamers left behind.

Every PlayToEnjoy Titans game is designed with blind and partially sighted players in mind, from the very first idea. You should never have to ask a sighted friend to get you through a menu, or miss out because a game only tells you things on screen.

In my games

  • Every menu speaks, through your own screen reader where possible, or a built-in voice.
  • Sound tells you where things are: left and right, near and far.
  • You can play with a keyboard, a game controller, or a touchscreen on a phone.
  • Games run on every level of device, from very old built-in graphics up to powerful gaming machines.

On this website

  • Every page has a skip to main content link, clear headings and landmarks.
  • Every picture has a description, and nothing important is shown only in a picture or only by colour.
  • Text and buttons use high-contrast colours, and the keyboard focus is always clearly outlined.
  • Nothing moves, flashes or plays sound on its own.
  • The pages work on phones, tablets and computers, and follow your device’s light or dark setting.

Tell me what is not working

If something in a game or on this website gets in your way, please tell me and I will do my best to fix it. Contact me.
