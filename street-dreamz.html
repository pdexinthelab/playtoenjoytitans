About the game

Street Dreamz is a street basketball game for everyone to enjoy, blind or sighted. It is built with blind gamers in mind from the ground up, so blind and sighted players can play together or on their own. 3D sound puts every player, the ball, the rim and the crowd around you, with spoken menus, a commentator and controller vibration.

Play one on one, two on two, three on three or five on five on courts in cities around the world, with the computer filling any empty places, or sit back and watch a game with full commentary.

This game is still being made, so there are no downloads yet. News about it will appear on the News page, on our Discord server and in our WhatsApp group.
