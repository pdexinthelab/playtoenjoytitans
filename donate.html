Donate

Thank you for thinking of supporting our work.

Every donation, big or small, helps us keep making games that everyone can play, so that sighted and blind gamers can jump in together or on their own. It pays for voice recording, sound, software and the time it takes to test every game properly.

Donations are handled safely by PayPal. This website never asks for or stores any payment details.

The Donate with PayPal link is being set up. Please check back very soon.

Not able to donate? Playing a trial and sending us your feedback, or telling a friend about our games, helps just as much.
