Every PlayToEnjoy Titans game is designed to run on every level of device, from very old built-in graphics up to machines with a powerful graphics card.

About the game

Serve, Love, And Point is a full tennis game for everyone to enjoy, blind or sighted. It is built with blind gamers in mind from the ground up, so blind and sighted players can play together or on their own. 3D sound puts the ball, every bounce, your opponent and the crowd around you, the controller's vibration tells you where the ball is heading, and a bell tells you when to swing.

Play a quick match, a world tour or a whole career, singles or doubles, on hard courts, clay and grass, by day or under the lights. Practise with your coach, take on drills and the weekly challenge, chase records and milestones, or play your friends online, one on one or two on two, with winner stays on.

Daniel the umpire calls every point, Isabella talks you through the menus and coaches you on court, and Dex and Noori bring the commentary. The players' strokes come from real tennis players' motion capture, and a full stadium watches every point: they cheer the big ones, and they rise to their feet when the match is won.

This game is still being made, so there are no downloads yet. It is coming to Windows first. News about it will appear on the News page, on our Discord server and in our WhatsApp group.
