Help

Answers to the questions we are asked most. Can’t find yours? Contact us or ask on Discord.

Are the games free?

The trials are free to download and play for 30 days, and our mods are free.

Can sighted and blind players play together?

Yes. Our games are full-fledged games for everyone, with graphics and sound. Sighted and blind gamers can play together, side by side or online where a game has online play, or on their own.

How do I turn the speech and audio features on or off?

They are built into every game, and you can switch them on or off from the menus. In most of our games F12 on the keyboard also switches the speech on or off. Each game’s page lists its controls.

Which screen readers work?

On Windows: NVDA, JAWS and Narrator, or the built-in Windows voice. On a Mac: VoiceOver, or the Mac’s own voice. On Android: the game speaks in your phone’s own voice, and explains how to use it alongside TalkBack.

Will it run on my computer?

Every PlayToEnjoy Titans game is designed to run on every level of device, from very old built-in graphics up to machines with a powerful graphics card. Each game page lists its system requirements.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Yes. Windows says this about programs from independent developers that are not signed by Microsoft. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Only download our games from this website.

My Mac says it cannot check the app.

Our games are not signed by Apple. Control-click (or right-click) the file, choose Open, then Open again. If macOS still refuses: System Settings, Privacy and Security, then Open Anyway.

How do I install on an Android phone?

Open the .apk file on your phone. Android asks to allow installing apps from that source (Files, Chrome or your email app): allow it, then Install. Our games do not come from the Play Store.

What happens when a trial ends?

The trial lasts 30 days from the first time you play, and the game tells you how many days are left. When it is over, the game says so and offers to remove itself. The trial installs beside the full game, with its own saves.

Why are the trials called betas?

Our trials are betas: early versions we share so gamers can play them and tell us what they think. Your feedback helps us improve, fine-tune and find any problems before the full game comes out, so every bit of feedback and support goes a long way. Each game page has a Send feedback link, or you can tell us on our Discord.

How do I uninstall a game?

Windows: Settings, Apps, choose the game, then Uninstall. Mac: drag the game from Applications to the Bin. Android: press and hold the game’s icon, then Uninstall.

I found a bug. How do I tell you?

Each game page has a Send feedback link that opens an email with the game’s name filled in. It helps to say which device and which screen reader you use.

Do you track me?

No. We count page visits and downloads with GoatCounter, a small open source counter that uses no cookies and does not follow you to other websites. It tells us how many people downloaded each game, and which country they were in, never who they are.

Where can I talk to other players?

On our Discord server.
