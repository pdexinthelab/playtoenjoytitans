Gizmo

Coming soon

An all-in-one accessibility suite for Android phones and tablets, made by PlayToEnjoy Titans.

About the app

Gizmo is much more than a screen reader. It’s a whole accessibility suite in one app: a screen reader, screen recognition, a camera reader, three keyboards of its own, voice commands and plenty more, built with blind and low-vision users in mind from the ground up.

Smoothness comes first. Gizmo is smoother than the forbidden fruit somebody else has already bitten, and better than the slowed old robot droid. Swipe from item to item as fast as you like and every move is spoken, in order, with no stops, no dropped swipes and no waiting at the end of a list.

It works on any Android phone or tablet from Android 8 onwards, whatever the brand, and makes full use of each device without slowing it down.

What Gizmo does

  • Smooth screen reading. Speech starts the moment you swipe, because Gizmo gets the items around you ready before you reach them. Fast speech stays clear without going high-pitched.
  • Your gestures, your way. Choose between two sets of gestures, and move by headings, links, lines, words or characters. Feel a light tick on every move, and give each kind of item its own little sound if you like.
  • Screen recognition. In apps and games that say little or nothing, Gizmo reads the words in the picture of the screen, so you can swipe to them and tap them.
  • Buttons with no name. When an app forgets to label a button, Gizmo reads the words drawn on it.
  • Read with the camera. Hold up a letter or a label: Gizmo tells you which way to move, then reads it out.
  • Faces on camera. While a camera app is open, Gizmo tells you how many faces are in the picture, where they are and how big.
  • Three keyboards of its own. Braille screen input, with contracted braille. Flex Type: tap with one to four fingers for each letter and Gizmo works out the word. And Pen Type: write each letter on the screen with your finger.
  • Voice commands. Say “What did I miss”, “Find Wi-Fi”, “Open Chrome”, “Read from top” and plenty more.
  • Notification history. Your last 50 notifications, newest first, even the ones that came while the screen was off.
  • Calls. Callers are announced by name while the phone rings.
  • Other languages. Text in another language is read in that language’s own voice.
  • Screen curtain. The screen goes black for privacy while Gizmo carries on working.
  • Bookmarks, labels and settings for each app. Jump back to the places you use most, name unlabelled things yourself, and give each app its own speech rate and more.
  • Settings that move with you. Save them to a file, or let your phone’s own backup carry them to your next phone.
  • Ready from the start. Gizmo speaks on the lock screen straight after a restart, so you can put in your PIN with speech.
  • Always a way back. If anything goes wrong, Gizmo hands back to your phone’s own screen reader by itself, and holding both volume keys always switches between them.

Gizmo is still being made and tested, so there is no download yet. News about it will appear on the News page, on our Discord server and in our WhatsApp group.
