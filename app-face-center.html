Face Center

Available now

Know you’re centred on camera, without having to see the screen. A free Mac app by Chris Douglas.

About the app

Face Center uses your Mac’s camera to find your face and tells you out loud whether you’re centred in the frame. If you’re off, it tells you which way to move, for example “Too far left. Move right”, “A little high. Move a little down” or “Too close. Move back”. When you’re in the right spot, it says “Centered”.

It’s ideal for getting set up before a video call, a recording or a livestream, especially if you’re blind or have low vision. It speaks through VoiceOver, or through the Mac’s own voice if you prefer.

Face Center is made by Chris Douglas, who shares it free. We are proud to share it here.

How to install

  1. Open the downloaded zip file, then move Face Center to your Applications folder.
  2. Open Face Center. Because it isn’t from the App Store, macOS blocks it the first time. Choose Done.
  3. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security. In the Security section, choose Open Anyway next to the message about Face Center, and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
  4. When Face Center asks to use the camera, choose Allow.

You only need to do this once. Updates install without these steps.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later). Face Center does not run on Intel Macs.
  • macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
  • A built-in camera, a USB webcam, or an iPhone used as a Continuity Camera.
  • A MIDI controller if you want one; it is optional.

Features

  • Built for VoiceOver from the start. It speaks through VoiceOver, or through the Mac’s own voice.
  • Speaks only when it matters. It only speaks when your position changes, and never more often than the interval you choose.
  • Works from anywhere. Its keyboard shortcuts work from any app, even with Face Center in the background: Shift Option Command C checks your position, and Shift Option Command S starts or stops the camera. You can change every shortcut.
  • MIDI control. Trigger any action from a MIDI controller with MIDI Learn, handy for streamers and musicians.
  • Fits your setup. Keep it in the Dock or the menu bar, and have it open when you log in.
  • Clear about problems. It tells you when no face is found, when a camera is disconnected or in use, or when camera permission is missing.
  • Updates itself. When a new version comes out, it asks whether to install it and does it in one step. You can turn this off.
  • Private. Everything happens on your Mac. It never saves photos or video, and nothing from your camera leaves your Mac. It only goes online to check for updates, and to send feedback when you choose to.
