Coming soon

Every PlayToEnjoy Titans game is designed to run on every level of device, from very old built-in graphics up to machines with a powerful graphics card.

About the game

Footy 27 is a full 3D football game for everyone: sighted and blind players can play together or on their own. The audio features are built in, with spoken menus through your own screen reader or the Windows voice, match audio and controller vibration, and you can switch them on or off from the menus.

Single matches, leagues, career modes and online play for two.

This game is still being made, so there are no downloads yet. News about it will appear on the News page, on our Discord server and in our WhatsApp group.
