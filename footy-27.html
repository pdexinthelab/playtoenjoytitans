Every PlayToEnjoy Titans game is designed to run on every level of device, from very old built-in graphics up to machines with a powerful graphics card.

About the game

Footy 27 is an accessible 3D football game built so blind and low-vision players can play on the pitch by sound and touch. The menus speak through your own screen reader or the Windows voice, and match audio and controller vibration let you follow the play.

Single matches, leagues, career modes and online play for two, spoken and heard throughout.

This game is still being made, so there are no downloads yet. News about it will appear on the News page.
