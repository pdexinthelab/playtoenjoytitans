About the game

PlayToEnjoy Titans Air Hockey is a full air hockey game that blind and sighted players can enjoy together. Play against computer players, work your way through a career, enter tournaments, or take on your friends online.

Full screen reader support, detailed 3D sound and audio description are built in, and you can switch them on or off from the menus. Play with a controller, a keyboard or a mouse.

How to install

Choosing the download link starts the download straight away. The file is large, so it may take a few minutes. Your browser tells you when it has finished, and the file goes into your Downloads folder.

Open the Setup file. Our games are made by an independent studio and are not signed by Microsoft, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer screens are standard Windows ones that NVDA, JAWS and Narrator read.

If you use the audio features, headphones are best: left and right matter.

System requirements

Minimum

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • About 4 GB of free space to install. The game itself takes about 2.5 GB.
  • 8 GB of memory (RAM).
  • A graphics card from about 2017 or newer that runs DirectX 11 or 12, such as an NVIDIA GTX 1060 or an AMD RX 580.

Recommended

  • Headphones.
  • A game controller, a keyboard or a mouse.
  • To play friends online: Steam (free), running and signed in. Steam also handles invites and voice chat. Without Steam, you can still play people on the same home network.
  • An internet connection only for online play, updates and sending problem reports. Everything else works offline.

Accessibility features

  • Full screen reader support.
  • Detailed 3D sound, so you can follow the puck.
  • Audio description.
  • Play with a controller, a keyboard or a mouse, against the computer, in a career, in tournaments or online.
