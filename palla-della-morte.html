Palla della Morte (Death Ball)

Available now

About the game

Palla della Morte, or Death Ball, is a brutal full-contact sport in packed arenas. Forwards carry a spiked titanium ball while bludgeoners swing bats to stop them. Score in three tunnels in the wall, worth 10, 25 and 50 points: the first team past 500 wins.

There are eight clubs, crowds of 100,000 and a broadcast booth calling every play. Run a club as its coach and general manager, or take on a friend side by side or online.

Sighted and blind players can play together or on their own. The audio features are built in: athletes call out from where they stand, every sound is placed around you in 3D, and every menu speaks through your screen reader. Switch them on or off from the menus. Play with a controller or a keyboard.

How to install

Choosing the download link starts the download straight away. The file is large, so it may take a few minutes. Your browser tells you when it has finished, and the file goes into your Downloads folder.

Open the Setup file. Our games are made by an independent studio and are not signed by Microsoft, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The installer screens are standard Windows ones that NVDA, JAWS and Narrator read.

If you use the audio features, headphones are best: left and right matter.

System requirements

Minimum

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • About 3 GB of free space to install. The game itself takes about 1 GB.
  • 8 GB of memory (RAM).
  • A graphics card or chip from about 2017 or newer that runs DirectX 11 or 12, or a gaming handheld such as the ROG Ally.

Recommended

  • Headphones.
  • A game controller or a keyboard.
  • To play a friend online: Steam (free), running and signed in. Without Steam, you can still play people on the same home network.
  • An internet connection only for online play and updates. Everything else works offline.

Accessibility features

  • Every menu speaks through your screen reader.
  • Athletes call out from where they stand, and every sound is placed around you in 3D.
  • A broadcast booth calls every play.
  • Play side by side or online, with a controller or a keyboard.
