About the game

Welcome to the University of Boujee Island, a prestigious college for blind students on a billionaire-funded private island. Behind the ivy and the yachts, a secret underground tournament runs for the billionaires’ entertainment, and the students are the fighters.

Boujee Island is a full 2D fighting game for everyone, so sighted and blind players can fight side by side or on their own. A commentator calls the action, and the audio features are built in: spoken menus, and sound and announcements that tell you where your opponent is. Switch them on or off from the menus. Play Arcade, Versus, Practice, or Story Mode: Dex and Noori, The Truth of Boujee Island. Fight a friend on the same computer or online.

With speech on, it comes through NVDA or JAWS when one is running, and the built-in Windows voice otherwise. Play on the keyboard or a game controller.

This game is still being made, so there are no downloads yet. News about it will appear on the News page.
