Super Video Golf Accessible Mod

Available now

About the mod

Super Video Golf Accessible is our unofficial accessibility mod for Super Video Golf, the open source golf game by Matt Marchant. It makes the whole game playable by blind and low vision golfers: every menu and every shot is spoken through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS or the Windows voice), with extra controller buttons and vibration.

With each aim you hear the hazards ahead, where a full swing lands with the wind and spin in it, and which club reaches the pin. On the green you hear the slope and how hard to putt. The swing tones match the meter, with five difficulty levels from Beginner to Champion. Billiards in the Clubhouse is playable too, and you can play online with your Steam friends, with voice chat built in. The speech switches on or off at any time, so sighted and blind golfers can play together.

New in Version 8: hole information as a list you can move through during play, with the hole’s shape and hazards, where your ball is, what lies between it and the pin, the green and the wind. Every club now says whether it reaches the pin.

This is a preview, so it is still being worked on and will keep changing and improving. The mod is not made or supported by the original developer, so please send questions about it to us, not to them.

How it works: three steps

  1. Buy Super Video Golf on Steam: Super Video Golf on Steam (opens the Steam store). The mod does not include the game itself.
  2. Install it in Steam and let it finish downloading. You don’t need to play it first. Keep it installed: the mod’s setup copies the game’s art, sound and courses from your Steam copy, which is not changed.
  3. Download the mod below and run its setup. It installs an accessible copy of the game beside your Steam one and sets up Steam, so pressing Play on Super Video Golf in Steam starts the accessible game, with speech, sounds and vibration.

How to install

Choosing the download link starts the download straight away, and the zip file goes into your Downloads folder.

  1. Unzip the folder: right-click it and choose Extract All.
  2. Run SuperVideoGolfAccessible-Setup.exe. The mod is not signed by Microsoft, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The setup is a text window that your screen reader reads line by line. It finds the game in Steam, copies its files (about 1.5 GB, which can take a few minutes) and sets up Steam. Steam closes for a moment and opens again; that is expected.
  4. When it says Done, press Enter to close it. Then press Play on Super Video Golf in Steam, or use the Super Video Golf Accessible shortcut on your desktop.

Updating from an earlier version? Just run the new setup over it: there is no need to uninstall first, and your profiles, scores and settings are kept.

To remove the mod: Uninstall Super Video Golf Accessible in the Start menu, or in Windows Settings, Apps. Your profiles and the game in Steam are kept.

System requirements

Minimum

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • About 1.5 GB of free space, besides the game in Steam.
  • The game’s own minimum: Intel 5th generation i5 or AMD Athlon, 8 GB of memory, NVIDIA GTX 1070 or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT graphics with at least 4 GB of video memory.
  • A keyboard or a controller (vibration needs a controller).

Recommended

  • The game’s own recommended: Intel 7th generation i5 or AMD Ryzen, 8 GB of memory, NVIDIA RTX 2070 or AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT with 8 GB of video memory.
  • NVDA or JAWS screen reader (the built-in Windows voice also works).
  • An Xbox-style controller, or a Windows handheld such as the ROG Ally.
  • A broadband connection and a microphone for online play and voice chat.

Accessibility features

  • Every menu and shot spoken through NVDA, JAWS or the Windows voice. Switch it off for a sighted player with F12 or both triggers.
  • Five swing difficulty levels, from Beginner to Champion.
  • With each aim, hear the hazards ahead, where a full swing lands and which club reaches the pin.
  • On the green, hear the slope and how hard to putt.
  • Hole information as a list during play: the hole, its hazards, your ball, the green and the wind.
  • Extra controller buttons and vibration.
  • Online play with Steam friends, with voice chat built in.
