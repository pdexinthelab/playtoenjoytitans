Super Video Golf Accessible Mod

Available now

About the mod

Super Video Golf Accessible is our unofficial accessibility mod for Super Video Golf, the open source golf game by Matt Marchant. It makes the whole game playable by blind and low vision golfers: every menu and every shot is spoken through your screen reader (NVDA, JAWS or the Windows voice), with extra controller buttons and vibration.

With each aim you hear the hazards ahead, where a full swing lands with the wind and spin in it, and which club reaches the pin. On the green you hear the slope and how hard to putt. The swing tones match the meter, with five difficulty levels from Beginner to Champion. Billiards in the Clubhouse is playable too, and you can play online with your Steam friends, with voice chat built in.

This is a preview, so it is still being worked on and will keep changing and improving. The mod is not made or supported by the original developer, so please send questions about it to me, not to them.

What you need

  • Super Video Golf, bought on Steam and fully downloaded. The mod does not include the game: the setup copies the game’s art, sound and courses from your Steam copy, which is not changed.

How to install

Choosing the download link starts the download straight away, and the zip file goes into your Downloads folder.

  1. Unzip the folder: right-click it and choose Extract All.
  2. Run SuperVideoGolfAccessible-Setup.exe. The mod is not signed by Microsoft, so Windows may say “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The setup is a text window that your screen reader reads line by line. It finds the game in Steam, copies its files (about 1.5 GB, which can take a few minutes) and sets up Steam. Steam closes for a moment and opens again; that is expected.
  4. When it says Done, press Enter to close it. Then press Play on Super Video Golf in Steam, or use the Super Video Golf Accessible shortcut on your desktop.

Updating from an earlier version? Just run the new setup: your profiles, scores and settings are kept.

System requirements

Minimum

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit.
  • About 1.5 GB of free space, besides the game in Steam.
  • The game’s own minimum: Intel 5th generation i5 or AMD Athlon, 8 GB of memory, Nvidia GTX 1070 or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT graphics with at least 4 GB of video memory.
  • A keyboard or a controller (vibration needs a controller).

Recommended

  • The game’s own recommended: Intel 7th generation i5 or AMD Ryzen, 8 GB of memory, Nvidia RTX 2070 or AMD Radeon RX 5700 XT with 8 GB of video memory.
  • NVDA or JAWS screen reader (the built-in Windows voice also works).
  • An Xbox-style controller, or a Windows handheld such as the ROG Ally.
  • A broadband connection and a microphone for online play and voice chat.
