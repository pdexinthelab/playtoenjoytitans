  • Palla della Morte: a sharper keeper and a fairer top basket

    18 November 2026

    A big update for Palla della Morte (Death Ball) today, with goalkeeping at its heart:

    • Every save you can reach is counted in: shots you can only reach by diving or jumping now get the same three beats as any other. Press save on the third beat and your keeper dives or jumps by themselves, with nothing else to press.
    • Never left guessing: a falling two-note sound tells you when a shot is going in that no save can reach, and pressing save tells you why nothing happened: no shot coming, too early or out of reach.
    • A simpler keeper's clock: your seven seconds now tick like a real clock, a little higher for the last three. The other keeper's last three seconds tick the same way from where they stand, and you can have the numbers spoken under Settings, Accessibility, Speech.
    • A fairer top basket: it is harder to hit from range, even for the league's best shooters, and the computer's keepers step out to cut the angle when a shooter comes close. It is still worth going for, but it no longer wins matches on its own.
    • Menus that click: menu sounds are now short clicks instead of metallic tones, louder, with a higher click when you change a setting.
    • Faster and steadier: matches start faster, and the game no longer freezes when you come back from a long pause.

    Goalkeepers, here is a tip from the new How to Play page: against a shooter close in, step out toward the edge of your crease. From there a jump can reach a shot at the top basket.

    If you already have the game, it offers the update the next time you start it. New players can get the free demo on the Palla della Morte page.

  • Boujee Olympics: hit the moment, your way

    16 November 2026

    Every jump, throw and hurdle in Boujee Olympics comes down to one press at just the right moment, and you choose how that moment reaches you. Pick your timing cue under Settings or Training:

    • Ticks and a bell: the ticks speed up, the bell rings, and you press on the bell.
    • New, a sound from right to left: a pulsing sound starts on your right and sweeps across in front of you, growing louder. Press the instant it is dead centre, then hear it glide away to your left. Best on headphones.
    • Feel it: on a controller, a buzz lands right on the moment, and a PlayStation 5 controller's triggers join in too.

    Whichever you choose, the timing is now rock steady in every event: the cues allow for your athlete still speeding up, so the moment always lands exactly where they lead you. And the javelin has a longer run-up, just like the long jump, so it is easier to time.

    The new trial is ready on the Boujee Olympics page: download it again to get the update.

  • Boujee Olympics: sharper timing on the bell

    15 November 2026

    We have fine-tuned the timing of the Action button, so pressing on the bell feels right on every hurdle, jump and throw:

    • The bell now allows for the delay of your TV, speakers or Bluetooth headphones, so a press on the bell as you hear it lands exactly on the board, the hurdle or the release.
    • Nothing is called a miss or a foul until your press has had time to arrive.
    • The last ticks before the bell are now a steady pattern you can trust, and the bell is louder than the ticks.
    • Key presses are timed to within a few thousandths of a second, and the starting gun allows for your delay too, so every start is fair.
    • Timing calibration now works with slower Bluetooth headphones as well.
    • In the full game, Steam now runs beside the game, so it can never take your controller away from it, and in an online lobby you can invite a Steam friend from a spoken list.

    After updating, please run Settings, Calibrate timing once, so every bell is spot on for you. The new trial is ready on the Boujee Olympics page: download it again to get the update.
